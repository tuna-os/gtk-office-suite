## Submitting your Adoption

If your organization, Linux distribution, or project is using GTK Office Suite, we would love to include you in this list!

To submit an entry:
1. Open a Pull Request adding your organization or project to the table above.
2. Include a brief summary of your use case and a public URL or contact reference.
3. Ensure your pull request follows our DCO commit sign-off requirement (`git commit -s`).

Thank you for supporting GTK Office Suite!
