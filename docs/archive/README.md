longer true. They are kept so their history and the issue numbers they cite
stay reachable, and each opens with a banner saying so.

[#1190](https://github.com/tuna-os/gtk-office-suite/issues/1190) (the road to
v3.0), the [readiness ledger](../readiness-2026-09/README.md), and the
[Render Parity Roadmap](../RENDER-PARITY-ROADMAP.md).
