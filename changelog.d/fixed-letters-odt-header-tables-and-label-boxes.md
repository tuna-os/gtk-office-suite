- Letters: in an .odt file, a header or footer set as a table now draws its text in the cell that holds it. A right-aligned "FINANCE BILL" in a table's last column ends at that column's edge, as in Writer, rather than at the right margin; on a page with wider margins it used to sit far left of Writer's. A classification label in a text frame narrower than its text keeps the frame's width and wraps inside it, as Writer draws it, instead of growing to one line.
